## Design & Build  
The DJI Mini 5 Pro boasts a foldable design that fits comfortably in your backpack or travel bag. - **1-Inch CMOS Sensor** for stunning 50MP photos and crisp 4K video at up to 120 fps  
- **True Vertical Shooting** support with 225° gimbal rotation allows creative framing for social media platforms  
- **Flight Time:** Up to 52 minutes using Intelligent Flight Battery Plus  
- **LiDAR & Nightscape Omnidirectional Obstacle Sensing** for safe operation in low-light and complex environments

## Intelligent or AI Features  
Equipped with DJI’s enhanced ActiveTrack 360°, the Mini 5 Pro intelligently tracks subjects with improved smoothness and precision. The drone includes QuickShots, MasterShots, Timelapse, and other creative flight modes that automate cinematic shots, allowing users to focus on storytelling without complex piloting.

The on-board LiDAR sensor, combined with multiple vision sensors, provides robust obstacle avoidance all around, including in nighttime scenarios. The drone also supports Non-GNSS Return to Home, memorizing flight paths to guarantee safe returns when satellite signals are unavailable.

## Performance & Durability  
Despite its small size, the Mini 5 Pro has a strong flight performance envelope, with max wind resistance up to 12 m/s and operational temperature range from -10° to 40° C. Its propellers produce relatively low noise, ensuring quiet flight for sensitive shoots.

The battery management system along with the Two-Way Charging Hub facilitates quick charging cycles for uninterrupted creativity. The drone’s O4+ video transmission ensures a stable and smooth 10-bit video feed enabling reliable control and monitoring.

## Safety / Reliability  
Safety is enhanced through the drone’s Nightscape omnidirectional vision system, combining binocular vision, forward-facing LiDAR, and 3D infrared sensors. This enables obstacle detection in all directions, even in dim conditions or narrow spaces.

The intuitive RC-2 controller with a built-in 5.5-inch, 700-nit bright display offers excellent visibility outdoors without reliance on smartphones. The controller supports dual-frequency Wi-Fi and Bluetooth for responsive connectivity.
